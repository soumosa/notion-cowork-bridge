# Code of conduct

This is a small project, so this is a short document.

## What I expect

Be straightforward and be kind. Assume the person on the other side of the
thread is busy, working with incomplete information, and trying to get something
done — because they usually are.

Criticise the code, the design, or the documentation as sharply as you like.
Don't criticise the person. "This breaks when the workspace path has a space in
it" is useful. "Did you even test this?" is not, and it's usually wrong anyway.

If you disagree with a decision, say why and what you'd do instead. I'd much
rather read a well-argued objection than a polite one.

## What isn't welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination on any basis, deliberately
derailing threads, or publishing someone's private information. Also: pressuring
anyone to weaken the security warnings in this repository, or to describe the
terminal as something safer than it is.

## Reporting

Email me at morankarsourabh@gmail.com. I'll read it, I'll keep it in confidence,
and I'll tell you what I decided to do. If a problem involves me, say so plainly
in the message and I'll take it seriously.

I may edit, hide, or delete comments, and block accounts, when it's the shortest
route to keeping this a place people want to contribute to.

— Sourabh Morankar
